Friday, October 22 / Summit Day

Go deeper. Follow your interests.

A day of focused conversations, practical lessons, and hands-on discovery. Each summit experience takes place at a separate venue in and around Vanderbilt University, Bitcoin Park, and Belmont University, all within walking distance of one another.

Ticket details to be announced.
01

Power of Payments

Explore how Bitcoin becomes everyday money. Builders and businesses share what it takes to make payments work, from Lightning infrastructure and cross-border settlement to the experience of paying at a local shop.

Explore experience ↗
02

Custody & Treasury

Go deeper into how individuals, businesses, and institutions hold, secure, and manage Bitcoin. Examine custody models, treasury strategies, inheritance, and the practical trade-offs that come with taking responsibility for your money.

Explore experience ↗
03

Global Compute

Explore the physical infrastructure behind AI and Bitcoin, from power generation and chips to data centers and mining. Meet the operators building across the world and examine how access to energy shapes where compute grows next.

Explore experience ↗
04

Capital at the Frontier

Explore how investors decide what to fund across AI, energy, Bitcoin, and emerging technologies. Candid conversations about investment theses, risk, time horizons, and the patient capital required to bring ambitious projects to life.

Explore experience ↗
05

Show & Tell

Get hands-on with what people are building. Live demos and open conversation give builders a place to share their work, get useful feedback, and meet people who can help move it forward.

Explore experience ↗
06

Grassroots Bitcoin

Connect with the people bringing Bitcoin into their communities. Exchange practical lessons on growing local meetups, supporting merchants, teaching self-custody, and building circular economies that people use.

Explore experience ↗
07

Alt Health

In partnership with Noble Origins

Explore how sound money and open intelligence can help us take greater ownership of our health. From nutrition and metabolic health to strength, recovery, and longevity, connect with practitioners and builders exploring how we live better, understand our own biology, and make informed decisions for ourselves and our families.

Explore experience ↗
08

Freedom Tech

Meet the people building and using technology to defend individual freedom. From Bitcoin and private communications to open-source AI, explore how activists and communities resist censorship, protect their privacy, and retain control of their money and data. Real-world stories meet practical demonstrations of tools you can use yourself.

Explore experience ↗
